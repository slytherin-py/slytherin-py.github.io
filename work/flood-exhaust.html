During the 2018 Kerala floods, vehicles stalled when water entered the exhaust. Back-pressure pushes water toward the engine. For an ambulance, a stall can cost lives.

  • Water enters the exhaust outlet
  • Back-pressure drives it toward the engine
  • Engine stalls mid-route

How I built it.

  1. 01

    See it firsthand

    In August 2018 I spent 25 hours over five days packing and loading relief supplies in Thrissur.

  2. 02

    Study the failure

    We traced how floodwater gets into a standard exhaust and stalls the engine.

  3. 03

    Modify the design

    We designed changes to the exhaust system that keep water out.

  4. 04

    Publish

    I presented it at the International Conference on Recent Innovations in Science and Technology (RIST 2021), where it won Best Paper. It was published in AIP Conference Proceedings by the American Institute of Physics.
