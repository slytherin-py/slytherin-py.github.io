Do industry-sponsored cancer trials enroll more patients than academic ones?

Role
Independent analyst
Tools
Python, pandas
Data

Outcome

Results

143,266Rows processed
3,446Phase III oncology trials
1.83xMedian enrollment gap
2Sponsor groups compared

Public trial registries are large and messy. I wanted a clean, defensible comparison of how sponsor type relates to trial size in late-stage oncology.

  • Raw export with mixed phases and conditions
  • Sponsor names need consistent grouping
  • Result must be easy to explain

How I built it.

  1. 01

    Load

    I loaded the full ClinicalTrials.gov export, all 143,266 rows, into pandas.

  2. 02

    Filter

    Kept Phase III oncology only. That left 3,446 trials.

  3. 03

    Group

    Grouped every trial by sponsor: industry or academic.

  4. 04

    Compare

    Compared median enrollment. Industry trials enroll 1.83x as many patients.
